<h2>Terms</h2>

<h3>QLogicae</h3>
<p>
    The designated group name for related projects. A blended word, composing 'Quandary' and 'Logicae'. The latter being the Latin word for 'Logic' while the former being the English word for 'Uncertain' - put them together in a meaningful way, you get "Uncertain Logic".
</p>
<p>
    For a one-man group (possibly multiple in the future) this does seem too far fetched or ironic. That's the point. Team names, or in the grand scheme of things - company names - are named to reflect the vision of the founders. In this case,
</p>


<h3>Aleph</h3>
<p>
    The designated project name. It is also the Latin word for 'Unity'. Makes sense since its purpose is to 'unify' all deployment software application related operations into one.
</p>
